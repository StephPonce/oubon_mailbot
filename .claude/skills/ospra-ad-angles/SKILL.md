# Ospra Ad Angles

Ads for one-and-done products must pay for themselves on the first order, so every angle has to earn a click AND a conversion. Lighting and home tools are the most demo-able category there is — lead with the demonstration, not the adjective.

## Angle library (pick 2–3 per product, never all)
- **Transformation** — before/after: dim → lit, cluttered → organized. The visual IS the pitch.
- **Problem-in-situ** — open on the annoying moment (fumbling under the hood, glare on the drawing); product resolves it in 3 seconds.
- **Spec-proof** — one concrete number, shown: "1,450 lumens — here's the garage at midnight."
- **One-cable ecosystem** — "everything charges on USB-C" as a household promise.
- **Scene ladder** — "you lit the desk, now the bedroom" (retargeting).
- **Gift / seasonal** — giftable framing; Q4 outdoor/holiday.
- **Honest comparison** — vs. the flimsy version they already own; no competitor names.

## Hook formulas (first 3 seconds)
"Stop [pain] — [demo]" · "I didn't know [product] could [surprising benefit]" · "[Number] that changed my [room/task]" · "POV: [scene] at night, then this" · "Things I regret not buying sooner: #[n]"

## Platform notes
- TikTok/Reels: 9:16, hook ≤ 3s, text overlay of the one number, native-feeling, CTA at ~15–25s
- Meta primary text: 1 hook line + 2 benefit lines + CTA; headline ≤ 40 chars
- Always produce 5 hooks, 2 scripts (15s + 30s), 3 primary-text variants

## Guardrails (hard)
- No health/therapy/medical claims (light therapy, pain relief, sleep cures) — describe function only
- No fake scarcity, fake countdowns, fabricated reviews or testimonials
- Every claim traces to a spec in the listing; if you can't cite it, cut it
- Respect platform policy on before/after for anything body-related

## Budget + kill thresholds (always include)
- Test budget: small daily cap per angle (default $20–30/day/angle for 3–4 days) and a total test ceiling
- Kill rule: pause an angle when spend exceeds 2× target CPA with zero purchases, or CTR sits below platform baseline after 2k impressions
- Scale rule: +20% daily budget per 48h only while CPA stays under target
- Derive target CPA from margin at retail and state the assumption. Never recommend spend without a cap and a kill rule — caps live in code, not prompts.

## Output format
`ANGLES CHOSEN (why)` → `5 HOOKS` → `SCRIPT 15s` → `SCRIPT 30s` → `META TEXT ×3` → `BUDGET + KILL/SCALE RULES` → `CLAIMS AUDIT (each claim → its spec source)`
